<objective>
Establish the Phase 18 foundation and prove the entire Aero-glass restyle architecture end-to-end on ONE component: `AeroSlider`. This is the tracer slice — it creates the shared `neutralRest` style factory and the shared slider style-resolvers, then wires `AeroSlider` all the way through (theme → resolver → M3 custom slots → tests green). Plans 02 and 03 expand the same proven pattern to `AeroRangeSlider` and `AeroProgressBar`.

Purpose: De-risk the architecture (neutral-vs-accent style split, glow-before-surface ordering, the M3 custom-slot hover-wiring gap) on the single most mechanically-involved component before touching the other two.
Output: `AeroSurfaceStyle.neutralRest()`, `resolveSliderThumbStyle`/`resolveSliderTrackStyle`, a fully-restyled `AeroSlider`, and two green Wave-0 test guards.
</objective>
