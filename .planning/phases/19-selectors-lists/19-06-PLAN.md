<planner_finding>
**Why the pill and focus boxes must change their sizing modifier, not just the row's height modifier.**

`AeroListItem`'s outer `Box` currently declares a fixed height, so its children receive a bounded height constraint and a fill-the-parent size modifier resolves to the row's height. The moment the row's height becomes a minimum instead of a fixed value, the outer `Box` is measured against the parent's constraints — inside a `Column` or `LazyColumn` that means an unbounded maximum height. Compose's fill-the-parent size modifiers are a no-op against an unbounded constraint: they fall through to the incoming minimum, which for a `Box` child is zero. The pill `Box` and the focus `Box` have no content of their own, so they would measure to zero height and silently stop drawing — the selection highlight and the focus stroke would both disappear, and every existing value-level test would stay green because the resolver still returns the right style.

`Modifier.matchParentSize()` is the `BoxScope` modifier that measures a child against the size the Box resolved from its OTHER children, which is exactly the semantic wanted here and is already the shipped pattern for `AeroSwitch`'s groove `Box`. The content `Row` keeps a width-only fill so it can determine the row's height.

This is a real trap, not a hypothetical: it is why Task 1 carries measured-height tests rather than only a source guard.
</planner_finding>
