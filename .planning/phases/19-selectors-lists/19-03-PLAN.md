<objective>
Turn `AeroSegmentedControl` from a bordered `Row` of flat tinted boxes into a Win7 toolbar strip: raised glass segments with exactly one visibly pushed in, where the pushed-in look is the Phase 17 pressed-button code reused verbatim rather than re-authored. Along the way the control gains real single-select semantics and its first-ever hover and focus states, both rendered strictly in-bounds because an outer bloom cannot survive the strip's own clip.

Purpose: VSEL-03 is a reuse requirement, not a rendering requirement — its real content is that the recessed segment and the pressed button stay one code path forever. That is what the visibility widening and the source guard in this plan exist to lock.
Output: `PRESSED_INNER_SHADOW` widened to `internal`, `resolveSegmentStyle`, a restyled `AeroSegmentedControl` with `Role.RadioButton` semantics and in-bounds hover/focus, and three green Wave-0 guards.
</objective>
