<objective>
Give `AeroSwitch` genuine Aero volume and its first-ever interaction states. The two flat `Box`es become a recessed accent groove (PRIM-08) and a raised, glossy, shadowed neutral thumb (PRIM-07); hover, press and focus are wired from nothing, following `resolveSliderThumbStyle`'s already-proven precedence chain rather than a third hand-authored one.

Purpose: `AeroSwitch` is the slider's sibling — accent in the groove, neutral knob riding in it — and is currently the only component in the eight-target set with literally zero interaction states. It is also the only component in this phase that legitimately uses the library's outer glow language, so it is where the glow-before-clip ordering rule has to be got right.
Output: `resolveSwitchGrooveStyle`, `resolveSwitchThumbStyle`, a fully restyled `AeroSwitch` with hover / press / focus / disabled, and three green Wave-0 guards.
</objective>
