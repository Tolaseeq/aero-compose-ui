<objective>
Close gap G3 for `AeroSegmentedControl` — put every segment label on one content token at full alpha, and make the recessed selected segment read genuinely pushed in by moving its value DOWN rather than inverting the label colour to compensate — and close gap G2 by gating the per-segment focus stroke on the focus-visible mechanism 19-05 built.

Purpose: VSEL-03 asks for "exactly one visibly pushed in". The shipped resolver reaches the recessed style by swapping the two fill stops, which preserves their mean value exactly, so on dark themes the strip reads as one uniformly bright block with no distinguishable selection — and the label inversion that was papering over it also dragged an `0xCC` background alpha onto a text colour. Both symptoms have one root: the fill never moved downward.
Output: a single-token label, a documented local darken on the recessed fill, an updated anti-drift test plus a new luminance-direction test, and the focus-visible gate.
</objective>

<planner_finding>
**Why swapping the fill stops cannot express depth on its own — the arithmetic behind G3.**

`AeroSurfaceStyle.rest(colors)` sets `fillTop = primary.lighten(0.08f)` and `fillBottom = primary.darken(0.12f)`. `pressedRecess` exchanges the two stops (plus the two bevel tones), disables gloss and attaches the inner shadow. Exchanging two values leaves their mean untouched: the recessed segment therefore has exactly the same average value as its raised neighbours, and differs only in gradient direction, the missing gloss band, and a 2.dp inner shadow. Side by side at 28.dp tall on a light-primary theme, that is not a readable difference — which is precisely what the reviewer reported ("the strip reads as one uniformly bright block").

`ColorMath.darken(amount)` multiplies each RGB channel by `(1 - amount)` and preserves alpha, so applying it to BOTH recessed stops scales the whole recessed fill downward by a constant factor while leaving the gradient direction, the bevel swap, the gloss suppression and the inner shadow exactly as `pressedRecess` produced them. The total luminance of the recessed pair is then strictly below the raised pair's for every scheme — a property the new test asserts rather than eyeballs.

`0.20f` is chosen to match `AeroButtonSurface`'s own `FILLED_FILL_TOP_DARKEN`, so the recessed segment lands in the same value neighbourhood as the pressed `AeroButton` the reviewer is asked to compare it against. The exact magnitude remains Claude's discretion per 19-CONTEXT.md and may be retuned at the 19-08 sign-off; the DIRECTION is not discretionary.
</planner_finding>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| user input device → focus indication | Pointer and keyboard events reach each segment's own interaction source and decide whether the in-bounds focus stroke is DRAWN. No other boundary is crossed: local desktop rendering, no network, no filesystem writes, no persistence, no untrusted input. |
| theme tokens → rendered contrast | The label token and the recessed fill together determine whether a selected segment's text is legible. A wrong pairing is a usability defect, not a data-integrity one. |
