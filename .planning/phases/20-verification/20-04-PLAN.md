<objective>
Close the folded `AeroButton` label-contrast todo (D-12 / D-13): the label has never actually been
chosen for contrast against the button's own fill. `AeroButtonSurface`'s `Text` sets no `color =`,
so it resolves to ambient `LocalContentColor` (`onBackground`, byte-identical to `onSurface` in all
three schemes). Measured against the button's own rest fill that lands at AeroBlue 2.35 / 3.49,
AeroDark 1.70 / 2.56, Classic 3.98 / 5.54 — short of the WCAG 4.5:1 normal-text floor on at least
one stop in every theme, and on both stops in two of them.

The fix is a **mechanism, not a constant**: one shared function resolves the label colour
algorithmically from the resolved fill, and both `AeroButtonSurface`'s `Text` and
`AeroSegmentedControl`'s segment `Text` call it identically. That is what D-12's one-source-of-truth
rule requires, and it is exactly why gap G5 was rejected at 19-12 — the segmented control's raised
fill had been darkened to 0.45/0.61 chasing contrast until it became a bespoke colour unlike the
button's. Per-component constant retuning is forbidden.

Purpose: this fix is folded into Phase 20 specifically because it is a visual change to a
Phase-17-signed-off component and therefore needs a fresh three-theme sign-off — and Phase 20 IS
that sign-off. It lands here at its cheapest moment.

Output: `resolveLabelColor` in `AeroButtonSurface.kt`, its two call sites, a value-level regression
test, and the two source guards updated so they gate the new mechanism instead of the retired one.
</objective>

<planner_assumptions>
- **Candidate colours are pure black and pure white.** The UI-SPEC contract point 1 words them as
  "a near-black and a near-white token", while its own pre-computed contrast table (point 2) and its
  point 5 ("a hard-black or hard-white label on a colourful gradient fill reads as a standard
  flat/native OS button label — this is the expected, intended look") were both computed and argued
  against pure `Color.Black` / `Color.White`. This plan uses the pure values, held behind two named
  constants so a later shift toward off-black/off-white is a one-line change behind the same
  function. Using anything else would invalidate the UI-SPEC's own table, and AeroBlue's fillBottom
  margin there is only 0.06 wide.
- **AeroBlue's fillBottom margin (4.56 against black) is a HAND estimate, not a measured value** —
  the UI-SPEC flags it as needing programmatic confirmation. D-13's test is the authority; Task 2
  runs it against the real `Color.darken()` RGB-mix math before the fix is considered done.
- **Mid-tween label flip on the segmented control is accepted and routed to sign-off.** The segment's
  label resolves from the animated/lerped fill, so during the 150ms selection tween the label can
  flip once as the fill crosses the contrast midpoint. This matches how the segment's gloss, bevel,
  rim and inner shadow already switch instantly with `target` rather than riding the lerp. Whether
  the flip reads acceptably is a visual judgment, recorded here so the 20-07 coherence pass looks at
  it deliberately rather than discovering it.
