Output: A ported, live-re-proven `AeroPanelGroupRecomposeUiTest.kt`.

DISCRETION (per CONTEXT.md): keeping the deprecated-but-compiling v1 `runComposeUiTest` OR migrating to a v2 API is acceptable, provided TOOL-03 and TOOL-04 both hold. Do NOT replace `waitForIdle()` with a custom coroutine-pumping shim (Don't Hand-Roll) — it is the dispatcher-agnostic idling primitive and should continue to work; add explicit `advanceUntilIdle()`/`runCurrent()` ONLY if the re-proof proves `waitForIdle()` alone is insufficient under `StandardTestDispatcher`.
</objective>
