  <name>Task 1: Three-theme × five-state human sign-off for both buttons</name>
  <what-built>
    Both `AeroButton` (filled) and `AeroOutlinedButton` restyled into genuine Aero glass through one shared internal surface: two-tone gradient fill + seam + proportional top gloss + inner bevel + outer contour at rest (VBTN-01); hover glow-ring + fill brighten, press gradient-inversion + inner-shadow recess (scale-shrink removed), persistent focus glow ring, disabled flattened-glass (VBTN-02); the outlined variant as a faint-glass fixed delta of the filled style (VBTN-05). Demonstrated in `ButtonsSection.kt` (17-04) with the live three-theme switcher.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. Run the showcase: `cd c:/1A_WORK/ui_lib && ./gradlew :showcase:run`.
    2. Navigate to the Buttons section.
    3. For EACH of the three themes (use the switcher to select AeroBlue, then AeroDark, then Classic):
       - Rest: the filled AeroButton reads as a classic Win7 blue default-action button — a real two-tone glass gradient with a soft seam, a top gloss band (~30–35% height), a subtle inner bevel, and an outer contour — visibly distinct from a neutral panel and NOT Material3-flat. The outlined button reads as a lighter sibling: a whisper of glass fill inside a brighter Aero contour.
       - Hover (mouse over each button): the whole button "lights up" — an outer glow ring PLUS a brighter fill — not just an outer ring, and the hover brightening does NOT bleed past the rounded corners (no square-corner overlay).
       - Press (click and hold): the button looks physically pushed in — the gradient inverts (dark-top/light-bottom) with an inner-shadow rim; there is NO scale-shrink.
       - Focus (Tab to the button): a persistent glow ring is clearly visible and distinguishable from the hover glow when both are active.
       - Disabled: the glass reads as genuinely "dead" — gloss and bevel gone, gradient collapsed, color muted — not merely a faded version of the live button.
    4. Confirm the long-label example truncates single-line at the locked height (no wrap/grow).
    5. Confirm the D-01 finding: the filled button's accent identity is present (blue on AeroBlue, theme-accent on the others) — accept the resolved-by-construction note that `AeroSurfaceStyle.rest()`'s fill/bevel/glow are already accent-derived.
  </how-to-verify>
