<objective>
Run the milestone's human three-theme visual sign-off (SHW-16), over the coherence section SHW-15
built — the last gate before v3.0 ships, and the one this whole phase is ordered around.

**D-01 makes it hybrid.** A cross-component coherence pass over all eight on AeroBlue / AeroDark /
Classic, PLUS a full per-state matrix for `AeroButton` and `AeroOutlinedButton` only. Those two are
the set that changed after their own sign-off: 19-09 moved their focus glow onto `focusVisible`
(WR-01), the G5 closure unified the segmented control onto their constants, and Plan 20-04's contrast
fix lands there too. `AeroSlider` / `AeroRangeSlider` / `AeroProgressBar` are NOT re-reviewed
state-by-state — nothing has touched them since 18-04's approval.

**D-02 fixes the DPI shape.** Two passes on one theme, AeroBlue, at exactly 125% and exactly 200%.
125% is fractional and is where 1dp contours, seams and bevels break on rounding; 200% is where gloss
and gradient proportionality show up at size. One theme suffices because the failure mode being
hunted is geometric, not chromatic.

**D-03 fixes the ordering, and it is load-bearing.** This plan runs LAST. In Phase 19 the sign-off
passed at 19-08, then code review opened CR-01/CR-02 and WR-01/03/04, reopening four requirements
and costing four more plans plus a re-sign-off. A verdict recorded before the gates and the review
are green is void.

Purpose: this is the phase's reason for existing. v2.0.3 shipped on a sign-off that was recorded
without the evidence to back it. The artifact this plan writes is the evidence.

Output: `20-SIGNOFF.md` plus its capture set.
</objective>

<planner_assumptions>
- **Captures are produced by the developer, not the agent.** The showcase is a desktop window under
  the developer's control and the DPI passes require changing a Windows display setting and
  relaunching; there is no CLI path to either. This follows the Phase 16 precedent, whose
  `signoff-capture/` directory holds developer-produced PNGs. The agent's job is to launch the app,
  hold the checklist, and record verbatim what the developer reports.
- **Two checkpoints rather than one.** The 100%-DPI theme passes and the two DPI passes are separated
  by an OS-level display-scaling change and an application relaunch, i.e. two genuinely distinct
  sessions. Folding them into one checkpoint would produce a single instruction block the developer
  has to hold across a reboot-scale interruption.
</planner_assumptions>
