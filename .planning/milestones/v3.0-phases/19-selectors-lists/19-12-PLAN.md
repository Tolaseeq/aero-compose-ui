<objective>
Put this gap round in front of a human on all three themes, and record what they actually say.

Purpose: three of the five items closed in this round are invisible to a test in the way that matters. Plan 19-10's darken has a contrast gate, but a gate at the WCAG floor says only that the label is above a threshold, not that the strip still looks like the component the developer signed off twice; and the arithmetic says the value needed to clear that floor is materially darker than the filled button this strip is supposed to read as a row of. Plan 19-09's focus fix and plan 19-11's emitter removal are proven by real input in a headless test harness, which is a strong claim about the mechanism and a weak one about the felt behaviour of a Tab walk. This checkpoint is where those become known rather than assumed.
Output: a showcase that actually reaches every state being judged, a recorded human answer per block, and either a closed round or a precisely-routed new gap.
</objective>
